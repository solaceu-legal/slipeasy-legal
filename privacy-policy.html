Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

SlipEasy ("the app," "we," "us") is a quit-smoking support app for iOS, currently available through Apple's TestFlight beta testing program. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and how it's stored.

Information We Collect

  • Craving log entries you record in the app — when it happened, what triggered it, how intense it felt, and whether you smoked.
  • Your onboarding answers — your current quitting status and roughly how many cigarettes you smoke per day.
  • Anonymous usage data — which screens you open and basic app-usage counts, collected through a privacy-focused analytics service (TelemetryDeck). This data cannot be tied back to your identity.

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, or advertising identifiers. SlipEasy does not show ads and does not sell or share your data with advertisers.

How Your Data Is Stored

In this beta version, everything you log is stored locally, only on your device. If a future version adds sync, it will be through your own personal iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit) — we would not be able to see or access that data ourselves. If you delete the app, your locally stored data is deleted along with it.

Third-Party Services

We use TelemetryDeck for anonymous usage analytics — see TelemetryDeck's privacy policy. We also use Apple's App Store and TestFlight infrastructure, governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

Your Choices

Because we don't collect information that identifies you, there's generally nothing tied to your identity for us to provide, correct, or delete on request. If you have questions about your data or this policy, contact us using the email below.

Children

SlipEasy is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app develops, especially as we move from beta testing toward a full release. We'll update the "last updated" date above whenever we do.
